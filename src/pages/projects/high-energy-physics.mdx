## High Energy Physics

High energy physics research spanning physics analyses and the development of reconstruction algorithms for current and future collider experiments, including the High-Luminosity LHC.

## Selected Work

- **CP Violation in $Z \to \tau\tau$** — Measurement of CP violation in the $Z \to \tau\tau$ decay channel at the ATLAS experiment.
- **Tau reconstruction with machine learning** — Development of tau reconstruction algorithms using novel machine learning approaches, including Mixture-Density-Networks for energy scale calibration.
- **Distributed computing for analysis and reconstruction** — Tracking as-a-Service and other distributed computing solutions, with work recommended for incorporation in upcoming LHC runs.
