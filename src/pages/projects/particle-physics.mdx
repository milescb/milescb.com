## Particle Physics

Experimental high energy particle physics, focused on $\tau$ leptons for both physics analysis and algorithm development at the ATLAS experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider.

## Current Work

- **CP Violation in $Z \to \tau\tau$** — A measurement of CP violation in the $Z \to \tau\tau$ decay channel.
- **Tau reconstruction algorithms** — Developing new tau reconstruction algorithms, including a novel machine learning model encompassing all tau reconstruction tasks needed for tau analysis, supported by the Department of Energy [Office of Science Graduate Student Research (SCGSR) fellowship](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/scgsr).
