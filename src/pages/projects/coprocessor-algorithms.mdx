## Coprocessor Accelerated Algorithms

Research on accelerating algorithms for high-energy physics by offloading compute-intensive workloads to coprocessors such as GPUs. This includes implementations of particle tracking algorithms with GPU acceleration and deploying them through service-oriented architectures.
